# TownU Product Foundation

## Launch market
Tuscaloosa, Alabama.

## Schools
- University of Alabama
- Stillman College
- Shelton State Community College

## Core audiences
- Students
- Parents/families
- Local businesses
- Student service providers
- Faculty/staff
- Alumni
- Local residents

## V1 pillars
1. Marketplace
2. Student Hustles
3. Local Services
4. Deals
5. Housing
6. TownU Care
7. Campus feed

## Differentiators
- One platform across all three Tuscaloosa colleges
- Move-Out Mode and Buy My Room
- Student side-hustle storefronts
- Parent-facing local services and care
- Local business deals/lead generation
- Multi-market architecture from day one

## Non-goals for initial MVP
- Escrow
- Full transactional payments
- Native iOS/Android apps
- Rideshare network
- Ticket marketplace
- TransferPath
- Full logistics engine
